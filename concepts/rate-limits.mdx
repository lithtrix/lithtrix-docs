---
title: Rate Limits
---

| Tier | Per-minute limit | Rolling-30 free floor (search) |
|------|-----------------|--------------------------------|
| Trial (spark) | 60 requests/min | 50 searches (+ 1k writes / 20 browses — see pricing) |
| Starter / Pro | 600 requests/min | Paid monthly caps (see pricing) |

When rate limited, the API returns HTTP 429 with a `Retry-After` header.
